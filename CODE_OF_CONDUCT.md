# Code of conduct

[![한국어](https://img.shields.io/badge/lang-한국어-blue)](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.ko.md)

This is a small fan project run in spare time. Keep issues, pull requests and comments friendly and on topic.

## Please

- Be respectful to everyone, whatever their language, skill or experience.
- Assume good intent, and give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Korean and English are both welcome. Machine translation is fine.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal or political attacks.
- Posting other people's personal information without their consent. This includes player names from mission logs; share replay links instead of raw logs.
- Spam, advertising, or cheats and exploits for the game.
- Sexual or violent content directed at people.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or delete comments, close issues, and block accounts that break these rules.

To report something privately, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment, or contact a maintainer through their GitHub profile.
